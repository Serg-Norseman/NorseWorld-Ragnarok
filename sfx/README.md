## Audio Copyright Notice & Disclaimer

NorseWorld: Ragnarok is a non-commercial, fan-made tribute project. 

The audio assets (music and sound effects) included in this repository were originally sourced from various open internet libraries and public archives around 15–20 years ago during the game's initial development. 

While we have made every effort to ensure the audio used is free of strict commercial restrictions, the original sources and licenses for files could not be definitively verified due to the passage of time.

**To Rightsholders:**

If you are the rightful owner of any audio material included in this project and object to its use, or if you would like proper attribution added to the credits, please contact me directly by opening an Issue in this repository. 

Upon verification, we will immediately either apply the requested attribution or permanently remove the asset from the repository and replace it with an alternative. Thank you for your understanding.
